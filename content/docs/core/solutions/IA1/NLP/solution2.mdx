### (Awarded 5/5 marks)

### Inflectional Morphology
<br />• In this type of morphology the meaning of the word remains same.
<br />• The tense of the word is only changed in Inflectional morphology.
<br />• **Example:-** dog → dogs, Cat → cats, Tall → Tall, kill → killed

### Derivational Morphology
<br />• In this type of the morphology the meaning of the word also change.
<br />• The whole meaning of the word is changed in the derivational Morphology.
<br />• **Example:-** faithful → unfaithful, kind → unkind, Teach → Teacher, Truth → Lie
