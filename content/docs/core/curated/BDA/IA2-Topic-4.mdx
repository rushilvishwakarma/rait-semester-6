# Euclidean and Manhattan Distance

(Topic content kept close to provided OCR. Only irrelevant answer-book fields removed.)

<img src="/images/docs/BDA/IA2/topic-4/diagram-01-euclidean-general.png" alt="Euclidean Geometry (General)" style={{maxWidth: '320px', margin: '0 auto', display: 'block'}} />

$$
AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2
$$

$$
AC = \sqrt{AB^2 + BC^2}
$$

$$
AC = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}
$$

Suppose in 3D we have $(x_1,y_1,z_1)$ and $(x_2,y_2,z_2)$:

$$
AC = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2 + (z_2-z_1)^2}
$$

This is the $n$-dimension Euclidean-distance style formula:

$$
\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i-y_i)^2}{n}}
$$

# Cosine Similarity and Cosine Distance

Used in recommendation systems.

$$
\text{Cosine Distance} = 1 - \cos(\text{sim})
$$

$$
\cos(\text{sim}) = \cos\theta
$$

Range mentioned in notes: $-1$ to $1$.

If $\theta = 45^\circ$, then $\cos 45^\circ \approx 0.707$ (high similarity).

Vector-angle representation:

<img src="/images/docs/BDA/IA2/topic-4/diagram-10-cosine-vector-angle.png" alt="Cosine Vector Angle" style={{maxWidth: '320px', margin: '0 auto', display: 'block'}} />

Special cases noted:

$$
\cos 90^\circ = 0 \Rightarrow \text{not similar}
$$

$$
\cos 0^\circ = 1 \Rightarrow \text{max similar}
$$

# Jaccard Similarity and Distance

Jaccard similarity indicates closeness between two sets $A,B$.

$$
d_J(A,B)=1-J(A,B)=1-\frac{|A\cap B|}{|A\cup B|}=\frac{|A\cup B|-|A\cap B|}{|A\cup B|}
$$

Given:

$$
A=\{1,2,5,4\},\quad B=\{2,3,5,7\}
$$

Without duplicate counting:

$$
A\cap B=\{2,5\},\quad A\cup B=\{1,2,3,4,5,7\}
$$

$$
J(A,B)=\frac{2}{6}=0.333\ldots,\quad d_J(A,B)=1-0.333\ldots=0.666\ldots
$$

(Original OCR had mixed decimal notes; the set operation above is the corrected minimal form.)
