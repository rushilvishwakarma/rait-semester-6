# PCY Algorithm (Park-Chen-Yu)

PCY is used for frequent itemset mining.

Apply PCY to the given transactions to find candidate sets.

## Given

1. Threshold = 3
2. Hash function for pairs: $(i\times j)\bmod 10$

Transactions (as in OCR sequence):

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_1&=\{1,2,3\}\\
T_2&=\{2,3,4\}\\
T_3&=\{3,4,5\}\\
T_4&=\{4,5,6\}\\
T_5&=\{1,3,6\}\\
T_6&=\{2,4,6\}\\
T_7&=\{1,3,4\}\\
T_8&=\{2,4,5\}\\
T_9&=\{3,5,6\}
\end{aligned}
$$

(Extra OCR rows $T_{10},T_{11},T_{12}$ were incomplete, so only complete rows are retained.)

---

## Step 1: Map single-item frequencies

Item set:

$$
\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}
$$

Frequency table from notes:

| Item | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Count | 7 | 6 | 8 | 8 | 6 | 4 |

## Step 2: Reduce by threshold

Keep items with count $\ge 3$:

$$
\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}
$$

## Step 3: Candidate pairs with support (OCR-preserved set)

Selected pairs with support $\ge 3$:

$$
(1,3), (2,3), (2,4), (3,4), (3,5), (4,5), (4,6)
$$

## Step 4: Hash pairs into buckets

$$
\begin{aligned}
(1,3)&\to (1\times 3)\bmod 10 = 3\\
(2,3)&\to 6\\
(2,4)&\to 8\\
(3,4)&\to 2\\
(3,5)&\to 5\\
(4,5)&\to 0\\
(4,6)&\to 4
\end{aligned}
$$

## Step 5: Bucket mapping

| Bucket No | Pair |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | (4,5) |
| 2 | (3,4) |
| 3 | (1,3) |
| 4 | (4,6) |
| 5 | (3,5) |
| 6 | (2,3) |
| 8 | (2,4) |

## Step 6: Candidate set table (bit-vector idea)

All above occupied buckets have bit value 1 in notes and satisfy threshold for candidate generation.

Final candidate set retained:

$$
\{(4,5),(3,4),(1,3),(4,6),(3,5),(2,3),(2,4)\}
$$

Memory check in OCR: keep only threshold-satisfying buckets.
