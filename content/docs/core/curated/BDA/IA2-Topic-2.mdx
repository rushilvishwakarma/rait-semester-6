## PageRank Algorithm

$$
PR_{t+1}(P_i) = \sum_{P_j \in M(P_i)} \frac{PR_t(P_j)}{C(P_j)}
$$

### Iteration table from notes

| Node | J+0 | J+1 | J+2 | Rank Order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | $1/4$ | $1/12$ | $1.5/12$ | 1 |
| B | $1/4$ | $2.5/12$ | $2.1/12$ | 2 |
| C | $1/4$ | $4.5/12$ | $4.5/12$ | 4 |
| D | $1/4$ | $4/12$ | $4/12$ | 3 |

At beginning all pages are initialized equally.

### Sample iteration steps (as in OCR)

1. For node A, incoming page is C:

$$
PR(A)=\frac{PR(C)}{3}=\frac{1/4}{3}=\frac{1}{12}
$$

2. For node B, incoming pages A and C:

$$
PR(B)=\frac{PR(A)}{2}+\frac{PR(C)}{3}
$$

3. For node C, incoming pages A and D:

$$
PR(C)=\frac{PR(A)}{2}+\frac{PR(D)}{1}
$$

4. For node D, incoming pages C and B:

$$
PR(D)=\frac{PR(C)}{3}+\frac{PR(B)}{3}=\frac{4}{12}
$$

Sum constraint:

$$
PR(A)+PR(B)+PR(C)+PR(D)=1
$$

### Second iteration snapshot

$$
\begin{aligned}
PR(A)&=\frac{4.5/12}{3}=1.5/12\\
PR(B)&=\frac{1/12}{2}+\frac{4.5/12}{3}\\
PR(C)&=\frac{1/12}{2}+\frac{4/12}{1}\\
PR(D)&=\frac{4.5/12}{3}+\frac{2.5/12}{3}=4/12
\end{aligned}
$$

As noted: page with higher PageRank is considered more important.

## Clique Percolation Method (CPM)

For social graph and $k=3$ (3-clique based):

Sample cliques in the notes include:

- $(1,2,3)$
- $(1,2,8)$
- $(2,4,5)$
- $(2,4,6)$
- $(2,5,6)$
- $(4,5,6)$

Communities identified in notes:

- $c_1=(1,2,3,8)$
- $c_2=(2,4,5,6)$

Adjacent cliques are those sharing $(k-1)$ nodes.

### CPM Algorithm (as provided)

1. Input social graph $G$ and clique size $k$.
2. Find all $k$-cliques in $G$.
3. Build clique graph $G_C$: each node is one clique.
4. Connect two clique nodes if they share $(k-1)$ common vertices.
5. Find connected components in $G_C$.
6. Each connected component represents one community.
7. Output set of communities $C$.
