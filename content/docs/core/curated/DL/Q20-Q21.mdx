## Common Formulas (Use for ANY CNN Question)

| Layer | Formula |
|-------|---------|
| **Conv params** | $(k \times k \times C_{in} + 1) \times C_{out}$ |
| **Output size** | $\left\lfloor \dfrac{n - k + 2p}{s} \right\rfloor + 1$ |
| **Pooling** | No parameters. Output size: $\left\lfloor \dfrac{n - k}{s} \right\rfloor + 1$ |
| **Flatten** | $H \times W \times C$ |
| **Fully connected** | $(\text{input} \times \text{output}) + \text{output}$ |

#### Special Cases

- **Same padding** → output size same as input
- **No padding** → $(n - k + 1)$
- **Pool (2×2, stride 2)** → roughly $n/2$

#### Input Channels Rule

| Input Type | Channels |
|-----------|----------|
| Grayscale / X-ray / spectrogram | <Highlight color="orange">**1**</Highlight> |
| RGB image | <Highlight color="green">**3**</Highlight> |

### 20. Question — COVID-19 X-ray CNN

Design a CNN for classifying subjects as COVID-19 patients or healthy subjects based on chest X-ray images having width & height of 150 pixels each. The feature extractor uses 3 blocks of convolutional + pooling layers. Convolutional layers have (3, 3) kernel and increasing by a factor of 4, starting with 2. First two layers use zero padding. The pooling layer calculates average over (2, 2) pixels. Total four fully-connected layers are cascaded to predict output probability. Neurons in each layer are reduced by a factor of 4. Calculate number of trainable parameters for each layer and CNN.

---

## Solution (mapped exactly to question statements)

### **“images having width & height of 150 pixels each”**

$$
H = 150, W = 150
$$

### **“X-ray images”**

Grayscale
$$
C_{in} = 1
$$

So input:
$$
150 \times 150 \times 1
$$

---

## **“The feature extractor uses 3 blocks of convolutional + pooling layers”**

$$
\text{Block 1: Conv1} \rightarrow \text{Pool1}
$$
$$
\text{Block 2: Conv2} \rightarrow \text{Pool2}
$$
$$
\text{Block 3: Conv3} \rightarrow \text{Pool3}
$$

---

## **“Convolutional layers have (3,3) kernel”**

$$
k = 3
$$

---

## **“increasing by a factor of 4, starting with 2”**

$$
2, 8, 32
$$

---

## **Block 1**

### Conv1

(filters = 2, padding = same)
$$
(3 \times 3 \times 1 + 1) \times 2 = 20
$$

Output:
$$
150 \times 150 \times 2
$$

### Pool1 (2×2)

$$
150 \rightarrow 75
$$

Output:
$$
75 \times 75 \times 2
$$

---

## **Block 2**

### Conv2

(filters = 8, padding = same)
$$
(3 \times 3 \times 2 + 1) \times 8 = 152
$$

Output:
$$
75 \times 75 \times 8
$$

### Pool2

$$
75 \rightarrow 37
$$

Output:
$$
37 \times 37 \times 8
$$

---

## **Block 3**

### Conv3

(filters = 32, no padding)

$$
n - k + 1 = 37 - 3 + 1 = 35
$$

Params:
$$
(3 \times 3 \times 8 + 1) \times 32 = 2336
$$

Output:
$$
35 \times 35 \times 32
$$

### Pool3

$$
35 \rightarrow 17
$$

Output:
$$
17 \times 17 \times 32
$$

---

## **Flatten**

$$
17 \times 17 \times 32 = 9248
$$

---

## **“Total four fully-connected layers”**

FC1, FC2, FC3, FC4

---

## **“Neurons reduced by factor of 4”**

$$
9248 \rightarrow 2312 \rightarrow 578 \rightarrow 144 \rightarrow 36
$$

---

### FC1

$$
9248 \times 2312 + 2312 = 21{,}383{,}688
$$

### FC2

$$
2312 \times 578 + 578 = 1{,}336{,}914
$$

### FC3

$$
578 \times 144 + 144 = 83{,}376
$$

### FC4

$$
144 \times 36 + 36 = 5{,}220
$$

---

### Output (binary)

$$
36 \times 1 + 1 = 37
$$

---

## **Final Answer**

### Conv Parameters
$$20 + 152 + 2336 = 2508$$

### FC Parameters
$$21{,}383{,}688 + 1{,}336{,}914 + 83{,}376 + 5{,}220 + 37 = 22{,}809{,}235$$

### Total Parameters
$$\text{Total} = 2508 + 22{,}809{,}235 = \boxed{22{,}811{,}743}$$

## 21. Question — Music Genre CNN

Design a CNN for music genre classification (5 genres: Jazz, Pop, Rock, Classical, Metal) from a Fourier magnitude image.

| Layer | Configuration |
|-------|--------------|
| Input | 256 windows × 200 frequency bins (spectrogram → 1 channel) |
| Conv1 | 5 kernels of (5×5), stride 2 |
| Pool1 | Max-pooling (2×2), stride 3 |
| Conv2 | 10 kernels of (3×3), zero padding, stride 2 |
| Pool2 | Min-pooling (2×2), stride 2 |
| Flatten | — |
| Dense1 | 50 neurons |
| Dropout | 50% drop |
| Dense2 | 10 neurons |
| Output | 5 genres |

---

![CNN Parameters Formula #invert-dark](/images/docs/DL/MSE-QB/cnn_params_formula.svg)

### Step 1 — Input

$$\text{Input} = 256 \times 200 \times 1 \quad \text{(spectrogram = 1 channel)}$$

---

### Step 2 — Conv1 (5 filters, 5×5, stride 2)

**Parameters:**
$$\text{Params} = (5 \times 5 \times 1 + 1) \times 5 = 26 \times 5 = \boxed{130}$$

**Output size:**
$$O = \left\lfloor \frac{n-k+2p}{s} \right\rfloor + 1$$
$$\therefore H = \left\lfloor \frac{256 - 5}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 = 126$$
$$W = \left\lfloor \frac{200 - 5}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 = 98$$

$$\Rightarrow \boxed{126 \times 98 \times 5}$$

---

### Step 3 — Pool1 (2×2, stride 3)

$$O = \left\lfloor \frac{n-k+2p}{s} \right\rfloor + 1$$
$$\therefore H = \left\lfloor \frac{126 - 2}{3} \right\rfloor + 1 = 42$$
$$W = \left\lfloor \frac{98 - 2}{3} \right\rfloor + 1 = 33$$

$$\Rightarrow \boxed{42 \times 33 \times 5} \quad \text{(no parameters)}$$

---

### Step 4 — Conv2 (10 filters, 3×3, padding=1, stride 2)

<Callout type="info">
  $$C_{in}^{(\text{next})} = C_{out}^{(\text{previous})}$$
</Callout>

**Parameters:**
$$\text{Params} = (3 \times 3 \times 5 + 1) \times 10 = 46 \times 10 = \boxed{460}$$

**Output size** (same padding formula with $p=1$):
$$O = \left\lfloor \frac{n-k+2p}{s} \right\rfloor + 1$$
$$\therefore H = \left\lfloor \frac{42 - 3 + 2}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 = 21$$
$$W = \left\lfloor \frac{33 - 3 + 2}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 = 17$$

$$\Rightarrow \boxed{21 \times 17 \times 10}$$

---

### Step 5 — Pool2 (2×2, stride 2)

$$O = \left\lfloor \frac{n-k+2p}{s} \right\rfloor + 1$$
$$\therefore H = \left\lfloor \frac{21 - 2}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 = 10$$
$$W = \left\lfloor \frac{17 - 2}{2} \right\rfloor + 1 = 8$$

$$\Rightarrow \boxed{10 \times 8 \times 10} \quad \text{(no parameters)}$$

---

### Step 6 — Flatten

$$10 \times 8 \times 10 = \boxed{800 \text{ neurons}}$$

---

### Step 7 — Dense Layers

**Dense1** (800 → 50):
$$800 \times 50 + 50 = \boxed{40{,}050}$$

**Dropout** → <Highlight color="orange">No parameters</Highlight>

**Dense2** (50 → 10):
$$50 \times 10 + 10 = \boxed{510}$$

**Output** (10 → 5 genres):
$$10 \times 5 + 5 = \boxed{55}$$

---

## Final Answer

| Layer | Params |
|-------|--------|
| Conv1 | 130 |
| Pool1 | 0 |
| Conv2 | 460 |
| Pool2 | 0 |
| Flatten | 0 |
| Dense1 | 40,050 |
| Dropout | 0 |
| Dense2 | 510 |
| Output | 55 |
| **Total** | <Highlight color="green">**41,205**</Highlight> |

$$\boxed{\text{Total Trainable Parameters} = 41{,}205}$$

## Quick Reference (Memory Aid)

- **Conv** → small params (kernel × channels + bias) × filters
- **Pool** → shrinks size only, **no params**
- **Flatten** → just multiply $H \times W \times C$
- **Dense** → biggest param count: inputs × outputs + bias

> **Key insight:** The Dense layers almost always dominate total parameter count!
