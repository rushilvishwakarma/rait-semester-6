## Neural Network Forward Pass — Q13 to Q17

**Key convention:**
- Each column of X is one sample.
- If $\text{rows}(X) \neq \text{cols}(W)$, prepend a **bias row** of 1s to X.
- $Z^{[L]} = W^{[L-1]} \times A^{[L-1]}$, then $A^{[L]} = f^{[L]}(Z^{[L]})$
- With 3+ output rows → **Softmax**; with 2 rows → **Sigmoid**.

-1 & 1 & 0 \\
1 & 2 & 1 \\
-1 & -1 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
\quad
X = \begin{bmatrix}
-1 & 2 & 1 \\
1 & 1 & 0 \\
-1 & -1 & 1
\end{bmatrix}
$$

**rows(X) = 3 = cols(W) = 3 → No bias needed**

$$
Z^{[1]} = W^{[0]} \times X = \begin{bmatrix}

## NN Training — 1 Epoch Weight Update (Q18–Q19)

**Convention:** Activation = Linear ($\hat{Y} = Z^{[1]}$), $\Delta W = E \times A^{[0]T}$, $W_{\text{new}} = W + \Delta W$

### Q18: Output after 1 Epoch + Parameter Count

$$
W^{[0]} = \begin{bmatrix}-1&1&0\\1&2&1\\-1&-1&0\end{bmatrix}
\quad X = \begin{bmatrix}-1&2&1\\1&1&0\\-1&-1&1\end{bmatrix}
\quad T = \begin{bmatrix}1&1&-1\end{bmatrix}
$$

**rows(X) = 3 = cols(W) → No bias.** Forward:

$$Z^{[1]} = W^{[0]} \times X = \begin{bmatrix}2&-1&-1\\0&3&2\\0&-3&-1\end{bmatrix}$$

$$E = T - \hat{Y} = \begin{bmatrix}-1&2&0\\1&-2&-3\\1&4&0\end{bmatrix}$$

$$\Delta W = E \times X^T = \begin{bmatrix}5&1&-1\\-8&-1&-2\\7&5&-5\end{bmatrix}$$

$$W_{\text{new}} = \begin{bmatrix}4&2&-1\\-7&1&-1\\6&4&-5\end{bmatrix}$$

> **Parameters:** $3 \times 3 = \boxed{9}$

### Q19: Output after 1 Epoch + Parameter Count

$$
W^{[0]} = \begin{bmatrix}-1&1&0\\1&2&1\\-1&-1&0\end{bmatrix}
\quad X = \begin{bmatrix}-1&1&0\\0&0&-1\end{bmatrix}
\quad T = \begin{bmatrix}1&0&1\end{bmatrix}
$$

**rows(X) = 2 ≠ 3 → Add bias.** $A^{[0]} = \begin{bmatrix}1&1&1\\-1&1&0\\0&0&-1\end{bmatrix}$

$$Z^{[1]} = \begin{bmatrix}-2&0&-1\\-1&3&0\\0&-2&-1\end{bmatrix}$$

$$E = T - \hat{Y} = \begin{bmatrix}3&0&2\\2&-3&1\\1&2&2\end{bmatrix}$$

$$\Delta W = E \times A^{[0]T} = \begin{bmatrix}5&-3&-2\\0&-5&-1\\5&1&-2\end{bmatrix}$$

$$W_{\text{new}} = \begin{bmatrix}4&-2&-2\\1&-3&0\\4&0&-2\end{bmatrix}$$

> **Parameters:** $3 \times 3 = \boxed{9}$
